# Week 7 — Time Capsule 2
## Initial Idea

When I looked back at the stories behind these four objects, I realized that no matter what happened along the way, each story had an ending that felt happy and fulfilling to me. Because of this, I wanted my piece to feel sweet and warm. I decided to make it look like a piece of wrapped candy, holding these objects and their memories inside.
![Candy-shaped time capsule](Media/candy%20outshape.jpg)

## Why Fabric?

I chose fabric because it is soft, and some of these fabrics have been with me for a long time, just like these memories. I wanted to use them to wrap and hold the objects and their stories.

## Wooden Bracelet

For the wooden bracelet, I chose the harbor-print fabric. My friend gave me this bracelet on the day I helped her move. It was a very busy day, and the busy harbor scene on the fabric reminds me of that experience. I also think its colors go well with the wooden bracelet.

![Wooden bracelet overview](Media/wooden%20overview.jpg)

I made a frame with gold foil because gold is precious, and my friendship with my friend is just as precious to me. I will treasure both this bracelet and our friendship.

On the right, I placed a foldable paper box inside the pocket to represent the day we spent moving.

![Foldable moving box](Media/wooden%20box.jpg)

I also added a small “Smell Me” tag to invite people to smell the bracelet and remember its scent. For me, the scent brings back the smell of the warehouse and the memory of that day.


## Headphones

I chose pink fabric because the friend who gave me the headphones studies jewelry, and her work clothes are pink. She is always working on assignments, so when I think of her, I picture her wearing pink.

![Headphones overview](Media/wooden%20headphone.jpg)

I placed two hands in the background because wearing the headphones makes me feel safe, as if someone is holding my head. My friend also makes me feel safe because she is someone I can always rely on. When I am with her, I feel like I do not even need to think. The hands represent that feeling of safety and trust.

Inside the pocket, I placed the birthday card she gave me along with the headphones. She asked many of our close friends to write messages in it. I was really touched to receive everyone's wishes, so I felt that the card and the headphones should stay together.

![Birthday card](Media/wooden%20card.jpg)

I used wired earphones to represent my  headphones because they are easier to roll up inside the fabric. There is also another reason: when we first met, we often shared wired earphones in class, each using one earbud to listen to music together. Those moments were how we became close friends.


## Cat Tarot Cards

I chose dark blue fabric because my life felt a little dark during the time when I used these tarot cards.

![Tarot cards overview](Media/Tarot%20overview.jpg)

I selected three cards to represent my emotional journey during COVID, from feeling lost to gradually finding a clearer path forward. The Nine of Swords represents my anxiety and worries about the future. The Hermit represents my search for answers and guidance. The Star represents the comfort and hope I found, even when the future was still uncertain.

![Three tarot cards](Media/tarot%20card.jpg)

I tangled the twine together to represent the confused thoughts in my mind during that time. This twine also has a personal meaning: my grandfather gave it to me. He passed away because of COVID, and I wanted to include the twine as a way to remember him.
I also placed some beer bottle caps on the fabric because I often drank beer during that time. Strangely, after the pandemic ended and I got into college, I stopped drinking beer. To me, beer became a symbol of that period of my life.

## Peking Opera Hairpin

I used colored paper to tell the story of this hairpin. It was a gift from my dad for my ninth birthday. I later left it behind in the United States, but eventually got it back. Seeing it again felt like meeting an old friend after a long time apart.

![Hairpin overview](Media/hairbin%20overview.jpg)

I also included a few pieces from a 3D puzzle with Chinese cultural elements that my dad gave me for my twenty-first birthday. I think my dad's love for me did not change because I lost the hairpin. He continued to give me birthday gifts every year.
